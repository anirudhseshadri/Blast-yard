      How to play

      Last one standing wins the round. Win enough rounds to win the match.

      The basics

      • Drop a bomb. After about two and a half seconds it blows up in a cross.
      • The blast breaks crates and stops at walls. Anyone caught in it is out, you included.
      • Some crates hide a power-up. Break the crate, then walk over the power-up to take it.
      • A blast also destroys any power-up lying in its path.
      • One bomb can set off another, so chains are a good trap.

      Controls

      • Phone: drag the stick to move, tap Bomb. A Kick button appears once you have kick.
      • Online on a keyboard: arrows or WASD to move, Space for a bomb, K to kick.
      • One keyboard, four players: arrows + Enter/Right Shift, WASD + Q/E, UHJK + N/M, number pad 8456 + ./0. Press R for another round.

      Power-ups

      They last for the rest of the round and stack. Everything resets at the start of the next round. What you are carrying shows under the board.

      Special tiles

      • Water: you can walk through it, but you cannot drop a bomb on it and blasts stop at its edge.
      • Conveyor: the arrows push you along. You can still walk against it, slowly.
      • Teleport pad: step on one and you come out of its partner.

      When the clock runs out

      Nobody wins on time. The timer turns to CLOSING and walls start filling the yard in a spiral, from the outside edge towards the middle. Anyone standing where a wall lands is out.

      Keep moving towards the centre. The walls keep coming until one player is left, so every round ends with a winner, unless the last players go out at the same moment. That is a draw and nobody gets the point.
